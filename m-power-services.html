Scope, Rollover, and Project Thresholds

Hour rollover (Managed Services only): up to 25% rolls to the next month (10h plan: 2.5h, 20h: 5h, 40h: 10h). Rollover expires after the following month.

Additional hours: Standard M-Power $275/hour, Azure AI $330/hour (requires client authorization).

8-hour threshold: requests estimated at 8 hours or less can be completed under managed services; larger requests are scoped as project work (e.g., migrations, major app builds, enterprise governance, large Copilot/AI implementations).
